FORTHEGLORY: TORN CITY DUELS - single-phone prototype

DEPLOY TO VERCEL (from your phone)
1. On GitHub, create a new repo, e.g. fortheglory
2. Upload index.html to the repo root (no folders needed)
3. On vercel.com: Add New > Project > import the repo > Deploy
   (Framework preset: "Other", no build command)
4. Open the link on your phone. It must be the https link:
   motion controls only work over https.

PLAYING
- Log in with a Limited Access Torn API key (or play as a guest)
- Pick the gun to duel with, pick an outlaw or call out a Torn player by ID
- Motion: phone at your hip pointing at the ground, wait for DRAW!, raise, aim, tap to fire
- Touch: hold a finger down, lift on DRAW!, tap the outlaw
- Best of 3 rounds. Beat each outlaw to unlock the next.
